How to get started

  1. Add your restaurant, hourly wage, and tip-out rule.
  2. After a shift, enter hours, cash tips, and card tips.
  3. Check the calendar for daily net pay and Stats for weekly or monthly trends.

Backups

Records live on your phone. Before you switch devices or uninstall, export a JSON backup from the app and keep that file somewhere safe.

Contact
